I recently had the privilege of presenting my research on Architecting the Glass Network in Allameh Tabataba'i University—a modern paradigm that seamlessly unifies Cloud Computing, Edge Computing, and Distributed Systems into a zero-friction, highly resilient architecture.
As centralized cloud monoliths hit physical hardware limits and latency bottlenecks, modern infrastructure demands a paradigm shift toward a unified Spatial Continuum.
💡 Key Takeaways from the Presentation:
1️⃣ Distributed Systems Foundation: Leveraging horizontal scaling, consensus protocols (Raft/Paxos), and decentralized node matrices to eliminate Single Points of Failure (SPOF) and guarantee fault tolerance.
2️⃣ The Spatial Continuum (Edge vs. Cloud Core): Balancing ultra-low-latency (<10ms) edge processing for real-time IoT decisions with massive cloud core horsepower for AI model training and Big Data analytics.
3️⃣ Glass Transparency Layer: Achieving absolute operational simplicity by abstracting heterogeneity—masking distribution, failure, and migration complexities from both the developer and end-user.
